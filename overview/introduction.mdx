## Why Monolith?

Monolith stands apart from traditional DeFi protocols through its innovative approach to:

- **Flexible Debt Positions**: Choose between paid and free debt with different risk/reward profiles
- **Dynamic Peg Stability**: Automated mechanisms maintain stablecoin peg through market operations
- **Community Governance**: Decentralized decision-making with immutable protocol upgrades
- **Real Yield Generation**: Multiple avenues for users to earn through staking and protocol revenue
