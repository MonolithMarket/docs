# Paid vs Free Debt

## Overview

Monolith distinguishes between paid debt (interest-bearing) and free debt (non-interest-bearing) positions.

## Paid Debt

### Characteristics
- Accrues interest over time
- Requires active management
- Higher risk/reward profile

### Use Cases
When to choose paid debt positions.

## Free Debt

### Characteristics
- No interest accrual
- Lower maintenance requirements
- Different risk profile

### Use Cases
When to choose free debt positions.

## Comparison

| Aspect | Paid Debt | Free Debt |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| Interest | Yes | No |
| Risk | Higher | Lower |
| Management | Active | Passive |
