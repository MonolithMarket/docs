## Prerequisites

Before deploying a stablecoin, ensure you have:

- Sufficient ETH for gas fees
- Understanding of stablecoin parameters
- Access to the Monolith protocol interface

## Step 1: Access the Factory

Navigate to the Stablecoin Factory in the Monolith interface.

## Step 2: Configure Parameters

Set your stablecoin parameters:

- **Name**: Choose a unique name for your stablecoin
- **Symbol**: Set the token symbol (e.g., USDX)
- **Collateral**: Select backing collateral token address
- **Price Feed**: Chainlink-style oracle for collateral pricing
- **Collateral Factor**: Maximum LTV ratio (in basis points, e.g., 8000 = 80%)
- **Minimum Debt**: Minimum debt amount per position
- **Time Until Immutability**: Duration until parameters become immutable
- **Half-Life**: Interest rate half-life (12 hours to 30 days)
- **Target Free Debt Ratio**: Lower and upper bounds of target band (500-9500 bps)
- **Redemption Fee**: Fee charged on redemptions (max 300 bps)
- **Operator/Manager**: Optional addresses for parameter management (can be address(0))

## Step 3: Deploy

Review your configuration and confirm the deployment transaction.

## Step 4: Verify

Once deployed, verify your stablecoin on the blockchain and add it to wallets.

## Important Notes

- Deployment fees apply
- Some parameters (half-life, target ratios, redemption fee) can be adjusted by operator/manager before the immutability deadline
- Core parameters (collateral, feed, collateral factor) cannot be changed after deployment
- Monitor your stablecoin's performance regularly
