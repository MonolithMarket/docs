## Prerequisites

Before deploying a stablecoin, ensure you have:

- Sufficient ETH for gas fees
- Understanding of stablecoin parameters
- Access to the Monolith protocol interface

## Step 1: Access the Factory

Navigate to the Stablecoin Factory in the Monolith interface.

## Step 2: Configure Parameters

Set your stablecoin parameters:

- **Name**: Choose a unique name for your stablecoin
- **Symbol**: Set the token symbol (e.g., USDX)
- **Collateral**: Select backing collateral type
- **Initial Supply**: Set initial mint amount

## Step 3: Deploy

Review your configuration and confirm the deployment transaction.

## Step 4: Verify

Once deployed, verify your stablecoin on the blockchain and add it to wallets.

## Important Notes

- Deployment fees apply
- Parameters cannot be changed after deployment
- Monitor your stablecoin's performance regularly
