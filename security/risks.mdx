# Risk Disclosure

## Overview

Using DeFi protocols like Monolith involves various risks. This page outlines the key risks you should be aware of before participating.

## Smart Contract Risks

### Code Vulnerabilities
- Despite audits, undiscovered bugs may exist
- Complex smart contract interactions can lead to unexpected behavior
- Upgrades and migrations carry implementation risk

### Oracle Risks
- Price feed manipulation
- Oracle failure or downtime
- Delayed or incorrect price data

## Financial Risks

### Liquidation Risk
- Positions can be liquidated if collateral value falls
- Rapid price movements can trigger cascading liquidations
- Liquidation penalties reduce recovery value

### Impermanent Loss
- Providing liquidity can result in impermanent loss
- Volatile assets increase this risk
- Complex yield strategies compound impermanent loss

### Interest Rate Risk
- Variable borrowing rates can increase unexpectedly
- Staking rewards may fluctuate
- Protocol revenue sharing can vary

## Market Risks

### Cryptocurrency Volatility
- Extreme price movements can affect collateral values
- Market crashes can trigger mass liquidations
- Correlated assets can amplify losses

### Systemic Risk
- Interconnected DeFi protocols can cause cascading failures
- Large liquidation events can affect broader market
- Protocol insolvency can impact all users

## Technical Risks

### Network Congestion
- High gas fees during network congestion
- Transaction delays and failures
- Front-running and MEV risks

### Bridge and Cross-chain Risks
- Bridge failures can result in lost funds
- Cross-chain transactions carry additional risk
- Multi-signature requirements add complexity

## Counterparty Risks

### Platform Risks
- Smart contract upgrades may introduce new risks
- Governance decisions can affect protocol direction
- Team changes or disputes can impact stability

### Wallet and Custody Risks
- Private key compromise
- Phishing attacks
- Hot wallet security issues

## Mitigation Strategies

### Risk Management
- Use appropriate position sizing
- Maintain adequate collateral ratios
- Diversify across multiple protocols
- Stay informed about protocol updates

### Security Best Practices
- Use hardware wallets for large amounts
- Enable 2FA and additional security measures
- Verify contract addresses before interaction
- Test transactions on testnets first

## Disclaimer

This is not financial advice. DeFi involves substantial risk of loss. Only invest what you can afford to lose. Always do your own research and consider consulting with financial advisors.

## Emergency Procedures

In case of critical issues:
1. Stop all protocol interactions immediately
2. Monitor official communication channels
3. Follow protocol team's guidance
4. Consider emergency withdrawal procedures if available

## Reporting Issues

If you discover a security issue, please report it through our [Bug Bounty Program](/bug-bounty) rather than publicly disclosing it.
